India filing questions

Frequently asked questions

Can Simplicon file an Indian income tax return online?

Yes. Clients can coordinate remotely and provide requested records through the secure client portal.

Do you support NRI income tax returns?

Yes. We review Indian income, residency, foreign connections and the filing scope before confirming the appropriate return support.

Can you help with capital gains and property income?

We support returns involving securities, property transactions and rental income after reviewing the relevant statements and transaction records.

What documents are commonly needed for an Indian ITR?

Requirements vary, but commonly include PAN details, Form 16, AIS or Form 26AS, bank interest, investment, capital gain, property, business and prior-return records relevant to the filing.
