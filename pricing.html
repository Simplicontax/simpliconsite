Pricing questions

What changes the final fee?

How are listed fees determined?+

Where the source price sheet provided multiple amounts, the published fee uses the highest listed amount. We confirm the complete scope before work begins.

What can add to the base price?+

Additional states, businesses, schedules, rental properties, foreign assets, prior-year amendments and information returns can expand the scope.

Does the fee include portal access?+

Yes. Your secure request, document exchange, activity history and finalized documents are included.

When will I receive a confirmed total?+

After intake identifies the countries, tax years, forms and schedules involved.
