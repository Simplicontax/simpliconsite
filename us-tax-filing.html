Frequently asked questions

Can Simplicon prepare US tax returns online?

Yes. Clients can coordinate remotely and share requested documents through the secure client portal.

Do you support federal and state tax returns?

We support federal returns and applicable state or local filings after reviewing residency, income and filing requirements.

Can you help US taxpayers living abroad?

We support expatriate and cross-border filings, including foreign income and information-reporting questions such as FBAR and FATCA.

Yes. We help eligible taxpayers understand document requirements and coordinate an ITIN application or renewal with a return when required.
